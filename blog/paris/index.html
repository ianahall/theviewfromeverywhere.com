Europe

Paris

June 10, 2012

Oh, the City of Lights! Paris is one of those cities that, when you grow up in the United States, you hope to one day visit as an adult.&nbsp;I will say, Paris is as fun as it is beautiful. Kel visited Paris in college when she participated in a fashion competition in the Louvre, so she was able to show me around a bit. We stayed in a cool, trendy hotel called Mama Shelter near the Père Lachaise, where Edith Piaf, Oscar Wilde, Jim Morrisson and other great artists are buried. The neighborhood felt pretty chill, and seemed representative of what it might be like to live in Paris. The hotel was hip and fun! We eventually made our way over to the Eiffel Tower. Have you ever heard of it?&nbsp; Refuge Des Fondus is an amazing place.
